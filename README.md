# Alien Steel

**Alien Steel** is a simple **Satisfactory** mod that provides 11 alternate recipes across two HUB milestones:

- **Alternative Steel Production (Tier 6)**: five alts for steel-based and related industrial components.
- **Alien Technology Mastery (Tier 9)**: six alts for advanced electronics, quantum materials, and alien technology.


## Unlocks

### Alternative Steel Production

| Alternate recipe | Inputs | Output | Building | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Silicon Steel** | 2 Silica, 4 Iron Ingots | 5 Steel Ingots | Foundry | 4 s |
| **Electromotor** | 2 Rotors, 6 Steel Pipes, 45 Quickwire | 4 Motors | Manufacturer | 30 s |
| **High-speed Transformer** | 2 Circuit Boards, 2 AI Limiters, 3 Reinforced Iron Plates | 2 High-speed Connectors | Manufacturer | 30 s |
| **Magnetic Sheet** | 8 Steel Ingots | 6 Copper Sheets | Constructor | 15 s |
| **Ferroconcrete** | 6 Limestone, 2 Steel Pipes | 5 Concrete | Assembler | 4 s |

## Features

- **11 new alternate recipes** available through two HUB milestones.
- **No new items, machines, or buildings**. All recipes use existing content.
- **New resource pathways**, including steel-based alternatives for industrial components and new ways to make late-game alien technology.
- **Vanilla-compatible production chains** that can be integrated into existing factories.

## Installation

Install and enable **Alien Steel** using the [Satisfactory Mod Manager](https://ficsit.app/). The mod requires a compatible version of Satisfactory and the Satisfactory Mod Loader (SML).
